# CCNA Tutorials

A personal collection of CCNA study materials, networking tutorials, lab configurations, and hands-on practice.

## Topics Covered

- **Networking Fundamentals** — OSI model, TCP/IP, Ethernet, and subnetting.
- **Switching** — VLANs, trunking, VTP, STP, Port Security, and SPAN.
- **Routing** — Static Routing, OSPF, OSPFv3, and route selection.
- **IPv4 & IPv6** — Addressing, subnetting, and routing configurations.
- **WAN & VPN** — GRE tunnels, IPsec, and VPN configurations.
- **Network Services** — DHCP, DHCP Snooping, Syslog, NTP, and related services.
- **Network Security** — ACLs, IP Source Guard, Dynamic ARP Inspection (DAI), and BPDU Guard.
- **Troubleshooting** — Common networking issues, verification commands, and configuration notes.

## Lab Environments

The materials may include configurations and exercises for:

- Cisco IOS-based routers and switches
- Cisco Packet Tracer
- GNS3 and other network emulation environments

Compatibility may vary depending on the device model, IOS version, and simulator image.

## Repository Structure

Materials are organized by networking topic and lab, making it easier to review configurations and practice individual concepts.

## Purpose

This repository is maintained for personal learning, CCNA preparation, and practical networking practice.
